# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Players looking for a brief, touch-friendly match-three puzzle. Audience and play context are inferred from the implementation brief and remain open to confirmation.

## Product Purpose

Astra Grove is an original browser puzzle about matching pieces, building cascades, and reaching a stardust goal within a limited number of moves.

## Positioning

Its original identity is built around a night-sky grove, hand-drawn botanical and celestial pieces, and small sound cues composed in the browser rather than borrowed assets.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- An 8×8 board supports adjacent swaps, invalid-swap feedback, 3+ matches, gravity, refills, cascades, score progress, and a 24-move limit.
- Four-piece matches create row or column clearers; five-piece matches create a color-clearing wild piece.
- Players can win, run out of moves, restart, and mute sound.
- No external game-engine or audio packages are required.
- The product name and the 900-point goal are implementation choices, not user-confirmed long-term product decisions.

## Evidence on Hand

The game is a playable in-repository implementation. No external game assets, testimonials, or product claims were provided or are used.

## Product Principles

- Keep the puzzle playable immediately.
- Make swaps, matches, cascades, and end states legible.
- Keep touch and keyboard interaction available.
- Use original identity and generated audio rather than borrowed game assets or recordings.
