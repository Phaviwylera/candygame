Privacy notice

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Astra Grove is a casual puzzle game. The game does not require an account. It stores your level, coins, hearts and their recovery time, best score, and sound preference locally on your device.

Advertising in the Android app

The Android app is designed to show Google AdMob advertisements at occasional breaks between completed games. The advertising SDK may collect and use device identifiers, diagnostics, and ad interaction information to deliver and measure ads, subject to your consent choices and Google's policies. The web version does not request ads.

For information about Google's data practices and controls, see Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising controls.

Children and privacy choices

Astra Grove is not specifically designed for children. The Android app requests advertising consent where required. Parents or users with privacy questions can contact the developer.
